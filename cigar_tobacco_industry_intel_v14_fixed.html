What to Watch

🌱 Crops

Planting, acreage, harvest timing, yield, leaf quality and curing capacity.

🌦️ Weather

Hurricanes, drought, flooding, excessive rain, wind and unusual temperatures.

🦠 Disease & pests

Blue mold, black shank, viruses, mildew, nematodes and insect pressure.

🏭 Factories

New plants, expansions, fires, closures, moves and production interruptions.

📦 Supply

Leaf shortages, shipping disruptions, aging inventories and proprietary tobacco availability.

🤝 Ownership

Acquisitions, partnerships, licensing agreements and major personnel changes.

Intelligence chain: Field → harvest → curing → fermentation → aging → leaf inventory → factory capacity → blending → release. Earlier signals generally deserve more attention.

Supply Chain

01 • FIELD

Growing conditions

Weather, soil, irrigation, seed, disease, pests, acreage and field losses.

02 • HARVEST

Yield & quality

Harvest timing, leaf position, wrapper quality, moisture and curing readiness.

03 • CURING

Infrastructure

Barns, curing capacity, fuel, labor, humidity control and post-harvest losses.

04 • FERMENTATION

Processing bottlenecks

Fermentation capacity, labor, aging programs and changes in tobacco treatment.

05 • INVENTORY

Leaf availability

What is actually in warehouses can matter more than what was planted this year.

06 • FACTORY

Production capacity

Factory openings, expansions, fires, closures, staffing and production shifts.

07 • BLENDING

Blend decisions

Changes in filler, binder, wrapper, country of origin, proprietary leaf or vitola.

08 • RELEASE

Market signal

New cigars are the final visible result. Treat them as evidence, not the beginning of the story.

Analyst rule: when a downstream release changes, work backward through the chain and ask what changed upstream.

Alert Rules

These are the conditions I would treat as worthy of a prominent alert when we build the automated version.

🔴 Critical

Major hurricane/flood/drought; confirmed widespread crop failure; major factory fire/closure; severe disease outbreak; major supply interruption.

🟠 High

New farm; factory expansion; major acquisition; significant acreage change; curing/fermentation expansion; proprietary tobacco program change.

🟡 Medium

Harvest reports; disease monitoring; export changes; new tobacco varieties; new production facility; important blend or sourcing change.

🟢 Low

Routine releases, events, promotional announcements and ordinary limited editions.

Verification standard

Tier 1: government/agriculture agency, manufacturer, official filing. Tier 2: established trade publication. Tier 3: retailer, forum, social post or unverified report. Tier 3 should never become a major alert without confirmation.
